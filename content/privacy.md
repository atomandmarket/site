This notice explains how personal data are handled on atomandmarket.com and in its newsletter, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, "GDPR") and Italian data protection law.

## Who is responsible

The data controller is **Andrea Gamucci**, author of The Atom & The Market. For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights, write to [editor@atomandmarket.com](mailto:editor@atomandmarket.com).

## Cookies

This website **does not use cookies** or similar tracking technologies (tracking pixels, advertising or social media scripts). Fonts, scripts and images are served from the site itself. Page views are counted with GoatCounter, which works without cookies and without identifying you (see below). For this reason no cookie banner is shown.

**Only if you click "Subscribe to the newsletter"**, the sign-up form provided by beehiiv is loaded inside the page. From that moment, and only for that purpose:

- beehiiv sets a session cookie (`_subscribe_forms_session`) needed for the form to work;
- beehiiv's security providers, Cloudflare (`__cf_bm`) and HUMAN Security (cookies and local storage starting with `_px`), check that the sign-up comes from a person and not from a bot;
- the form's font is downloaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address.

These are technical tools, strictly necessary to provide the sign-up you asked for and to protect it from abuse; they are not used for advertising or profiling. If you prefer not to load them, do not open the form, or write to me and I will add your address manually.

Links to other websites (for example LinkedIn or the original research papers) take you to services with their own privacy and cookie policies.

## What data are processed, and why

**Visiting the website.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. When you visit a page, GitHub's servers automatically process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, in order to deliver the page and keep the service secure. I do not receive or use these data to identify or profile visitors. Legal basis: legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

**Visitor statistics.** To know how many people read each page and where they come from, I use [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com), an open-source analytics service. When a page loads, it records the page address, the referring site, browser and operating system, screen size and approximate country, and shows them to me only as aggregate counts. It does not use cookies and does not save your IP address in its database (it is kept in memory for a few hours at most, only to avoid counting the same visit twice); it does not build profiles. Legal basis: legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). To stop being counted in your browser, open [atomandmarket.com/#toggle-goatcounter](/#toggle-goatcounter). See the [GoatCounter privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy).

**Newsletter.** If you subscribe, your email address is collected and stored by beehiiv, Inc., the platform that sends the newsletter, and used only to send you the newsletter. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email. beehiiv may record whether an email is opened and which links are clicked; I use this only in aggregate, to understand which topics interest readers. See the [beehiiv Privacy Policy](https://www.beehiiv.com/privacy).

**Writing to me.** If you email me, I use your address and the content of your message only to reply and follow up. Legal basis: your request and my legitimate interest in answering it (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

Your data are never sold, and are not used for automated decisions or profiling.

## Who else receives the data

Only the service providers named above, acting as data processors: **GitHub, Inc.** (website hosting), **GoatCounter** (visitor statistics), **beehiiv, Inc.** (newsletter and sign-up form, with its security providers Cloudflare and HUMAN Security, and Google Fonts for the form's font), and the email provider used for the contact address. Several of these providers are based in the United States; transfers of personal data outside the European Economic Area rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## How long data are kept

- Newsletter: until you unsubscribe, then deleted or anonymised.
- Emails: as long as needed to handle your request, and in any case no longer than two years after the last exchange.
- Technical data on GitHub's servers: according to GitHub's retention policy.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to their processing, and to receive them in a portable format. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time, without affecting what was done before. To exercise these rights, write to [editor@atomandmarket.com](mailto:editor@atomandmarket.com).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Italy, the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it), or the authority of the EU country where you live or work.
